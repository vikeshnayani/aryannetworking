Common questions

Before you get in touch

Do you work with businesses of our size?

We work with single-office operations through to multi-site enterprises. The engagement model changes with scale — a smaller business usually starts with a project or an AMC, a larger one with a managed service — but the design discipline is the same either way.

Will you quote without visiting our site?

For anything beyond a like-for-like replacement, no. Infrastructure quoted from a phone call tends to miss the things that cost money later — cable routes, power, existing licensing, how the building is actually used. The first assessment visit is free.

Are you tied to particular hardware brands?

No. We stay deliberately vendor-neutral and specify on fit first, cost second. If the honest answer is that your existing kit is fine and you do not need to spend, that is the answer you will get.

Can you support infrastructure someone else installed?

Yes, and it is a common starting point. We begin with an assessment to document what is actually in place, flag anything unsafe or unsupported, and then agree what we can take responsibility for under an SLA.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Hyderabad and work across India, with on-site response for clients in and around the city. Multi-site engagements further afield are handled case by case — ask us.
