Careers

We are a small, technical team. If you like owning a problem end to end rather than passing it along, you will fit here.

Why join us

Real infrastructure, real ownership

Our engineers work across the whole stack — cabling one week, firewall migration the next. You will see designs through from assessment to handover instead of being handed a ticket queue.

  • Hands-on exposure across networking, security, cloud and data centre
  • Support for vendor certifications and training
  • Direct client interaction from early in your career
  • A team small enough that your work is visible
