01 — Connectivity

Enterprise Networking

The network is the one system every other system depends on. We design switching, routing and wireless that stays predictable under load, and that you can extend without re-architecting.

  • Core, distribution and access switching design
  • Enterprise Wi-Fi with RF survey and heat mapping
  • Routing, SD-WAN and multi-branch connectivity
  • VLAN segmentation and QoS for voice and video
  • Network monitoring and capacity reporting

Typical outcomes

  • Consistent Wi-Fi coverage across floors and warehouses
  • Branch sites brought onto a single managed topology
  • Clear visibility of link utilisation before it becomes a problem
  • Documented topology your team can actually work from
