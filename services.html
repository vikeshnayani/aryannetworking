Services

Once it is built, it has to stay running. Our managed services keep the infrastructure monitored, patched and supported under a clear SLA.

Infrastructure Management

Day-to-day monitoring and maintenance of servers, storage and network devices. We keep the estate optimised so business services stay stable and highly available, and we tell you what needs attention before it becomes an outage.

Remote Infrastructure Management

Remote management of your IT infrastructure — workstations, servers, network devices, storage and security appliances — handled from our operations desk. You get continuous coverage without carrying the headcount on site.

IT Service Management

Unified management of client and server endpoints, run through proper process: incident management, change management, a real service desk and continuous improvement — measured on resolution time, not on tickets closed.

Infrastructure Deployment

From campus networks to data centre compute, our engineers handle installation, configuration, migration and commissioning — including out-of-hours cutovers when the change window demands it.

Data Centre & Automation

Server health monitoring, virtualisation management, storage optimisation, backup verification and infrastructure automation — all under a single SLA rather than split across three vendors.

Annual Maintenance & Support

Comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC options covering hardware support, spares, preventive maintenance visits and on-site response, with defined turnaround commitments per site.
